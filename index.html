SYSTEM PROTOCOLS & DOCUMENTATION

1. TELEMETRY MONITORING

• Liquid Cash: Unallocated capital ready for wagers or asset purchases.
• Net Worth: Total value of Liquid Cash + current value of all active Asset Holdings.
• Peak ATH: Highest historical Net Worth achieved during this terminal session.
• Assets Held: Aggregate total number of market asset units currently owned.

2. GAMBLING FLOOR PROTOCOLS

• Cyber Coin Flip: 50/50 probability. Win pays 1:1 payout on initial wager.
